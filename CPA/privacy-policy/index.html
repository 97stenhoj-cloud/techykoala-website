Privacy Policy

CPA - Content Posting Automation for TikTok

Last updated: April 1, 2026

At TechyKoala ("we," "our," or "us"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use the CPA (Content Posting Automation) service ("Service"), which enables automatic posting of content to TikTok on your behalf.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account registration details (name, email address)
  • Video content and associated metadata (captions, hashtags, descriptions) that you submit for posting
  • Posting schedules and preferences you configure
  • Communications you send to us (support requests, feedback)

1.2 Information from TikTok

When you authorize the Service to connect to your TikTok account, we receive the following information through TikTok's API, based on the permissions you grant:

  • TikTok user ID and display name
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens
  • Post publishing status and confirmation details

1.3 Information Collected Automatically

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, access times)
  • Service usage data (posting history, feature usage)
  • Device information (operating system, device type)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide the Service, including posting content to TikTok on your behalf
  • Manage your posting schedules and content queue
  • Authenticate your TikTok account and maintain API access
  • Communicate with you about service updates, issues, or support requests
  • Monitor and improve the performance and reliability of the Service
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 TikTok

Your content (videos, captions, hashtags) is transmitted to TikTok through their Content Posting API for the purpose of publishing it to your account. This is the core function of the Service and only occurs with your authorization.

3.2 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating the Service (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics). These providers are bound by confidentiality agreements and may only use your data to perform services on our behalf.

3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of TechyKoala, our users, or the public.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of API access tokens and sensitive data at rest and in transit
  • Secure storage of authentication credentials
  • Regular security reviews and updates
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data

Video content submitted for posting is stored temporarily and deleted after successful publication or after a reasonable retention period.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active and for a reasonable period after deletion
  • Video content: Deleted after successful posting to TikTok or within 30 days if posting fails
  • Access tokens: Retained while your TikTok account is connected; deleted upon disconnection or revocation
  • Log data: Retained for up to 12 months for security and troubleshooting purposes

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing of your personal data
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw your consent at any time by revoking TikTok API access or contacting us

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at support@techykoala.com.

7. TikTok API Data Usage

In compliance with TikTok's Developer Terms:

  • We only access TikTok data that is necessary to provide the Service
  • We do not use TikTok data for purposes unrelated to the Service
  • We do not sell, license, or otherwise commercialize TikTok user data
  • We do not use TikTok data to build user profiles for advertising purposes
  • You may revoke our access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's settings

8. Cookies and Tracking

The Service may use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session, remember your preferences, and analyze usage patterns. You can control cookie settings through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of the Service.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete that information promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. When we transfer data internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
