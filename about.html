Our Vision

We're a couple based in Copenhagen, both engineers by training, who kept running into the same frustration: the tools we wanted to use either didn't exist or felt like they were built for someone else. So we started building our own.

TechyKoala is our shared side project turned mission — creating smart, simple tools that save people time and help them focus on what truly matters.
